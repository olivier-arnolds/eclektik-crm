Your AI transformation is already running. Can you steer it?

It gets decided at the top and experienced on the floor, and almost nobody measures the distance between the two.

About the session

In most organisations the AI programme runs from IT or from a board mandate, and HR gets pulled in afterwards for reskilling and change management. That is late. Whether the transformation lands at all depends on what people experience of it, and HR is the only function that sees both what was promised and what arrived.

So we work through it as a loop you can actually run: measure what has changed in the work, read where AI landed as a help and where it landed as an extra burden, adjust the things that move the outcome, then measure again. This is a practical afternoon for leaders who are done watching AI happen to their organisation.
